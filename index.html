How To Play

flagObjective

Capture more boxes than your opponent by completing the last side of a box.

editMaking a Move

Tap any line between two adjacent dots to draw it — horizontal or vertical only, no diagonals.

check_boxCompleting a Box

When your line completes the 4th side of a box, you capture it and immediately get another turn.

linkChain Reactions

Completing a box can open up the next one — skilled players plan chains of boxes in advance.

sports_scoreWinning

Once every box is captured, whoever holds the most boxes wins the game.

lightbulbHints & Undo

Use a Hint Token to reveal a strong move, or Undo to take back your last move before your opponent responds.
